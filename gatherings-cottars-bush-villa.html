Why it is in the ledger

From experience

James has worked with the Cottar family since 2017. He inspected the villa himself and approved it for Gatherings because it solves the hardest part of a family safari: keeping everyone together without anyone running the house.

The villa sits on the Olderkesi Conservancy, 7,600 acres beside the Maasai Mara, where guiding is private and walking is allowed — something the main reserve does not permit. A nanny is included, so parents get their game drive. A private guide and 4x4 mean the family sets its own hours.

Questions, answered plainly

Before you enquire
Who is Cottar's Bush Villa for?
Families and small groups of up to ten (twelve on request) who want an exclusive-use house on a private conservancy in the Maasai Mara, with a nanny, private guide and vehicle, chefs, butler and housekeeping.
How do you reach the villa?
By light aircraft to Cottar's own airstrip, about ten minutes from the house; Keekorok airstrip is about 1.5 hours away by road.
When is the best time to go?
The villa is open year-round. July to October is the classic dry season; January and February are also dry.
Has James Wild & Co stayed at the villa?
James inspected and approved the villa and has worked with the Cottar family since 2017. An inspection is not an overnight stay, and rates and policies are confirmed at the time of enquiry.
