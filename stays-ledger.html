02

How to read a line

For travelers and advisors
Nº
The line on the page. Pages are filled in the order places earn their way in.
Plate
A photograph of the place, kept with the entry.
Mark
How James knows it: I Know a Place, I’ve Walked It, I Know the Host, I Keep Going Back, I Know Who Knows, I’ll Know Soon. Pencil means it’s in the library but not yet marked.
Nights
What James suggests to know the place properly. Stay longer and the house will often find you a night worth having.
Ledger ID
Issued once, never recycled. Advisors quote by it; the folio remembers it.
