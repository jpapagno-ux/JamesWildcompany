04

How it ties together

Five numbers, one book
  1. STY · ITN

    The Ledger

    James enters the place and stamps what he found. Nobody else holds that stamp.

  2. ADV

    The Advisor’s page

    Each advisor gets a number and a page. His marks go on it in green, beside his oxblood.

  3. Quote

    Quoted by number

    Proposals carry the ledger ID. The client sees exactly what was vetted, by whom and when.

  4. FOL

    The Folio

    The trip gets its own number, shared with him by link. Iggi, Safari Crux and Voyage Crux hand the client along by it.

  5. Return

    Back into the book

    His field notes are filed under his number. A good report can make a place I Know Who Knows. It never makes it I Know a Place.

05

House rules for the desk

Short, on purpose
He can’t stamp his marks.
He has his own ink. James’s six marks are his; borrowing them would be forgery, and we’d both know.
“Sent” means booked.
Quotes don’t count. Neither do clients who ‘loved the idea’. A guest is a guest when they’ve slept there.
His notes are signed.
Every margin note carries his initials and his number. Anonymous praise is how brochures are written.
The red pen works both ways.
If his page and his page disagree, one of them gets crossed out. Usually it’s an adjective.
