Why this exists

Built for advisors, not itineraries
For the trade

A safari that goes wrong does not cost us a booking. It costs the advisor a referral. Crux Safari exists so that risk is priced out before the quote goes out, not discovered in the vehicle.

44 years on the ground Advisor-ready routing

An advisor who sends a client to the wrong camp in the wrong month does not get a second chance at that client's trust. We built Wild & Co Safaris, and Crux Safari underneath it, so that call is never the one that has to be made.

Confidence, on a safari, is not a mood. It is a guide who has been in the vehicle before, a camp booked in season, and a route already stress-tested against the ledger rather than against a brochure map.

That is what an advisor is actually buying when they book with us — not a seat on a game drive, but the assurance that someone already checked.

“Africa doesn’t do encores. Every sighting is a premiere, which is why I keep buying tickets.”

James Papagno · On Wild & Co Safaris

Four ways to do it

The tiers
01

Privately guidedYour guide, your vehicle, your pace

One guide with you for the whole journey, in a vehicle nobody else shares. We match the guide to the traveller before we match the camp — a birder and a first-timer want completely different people in the front seat. Camps are chosen around the guide's routing rather than the other way round.

Sole-use vehicleNamed guide6–14 nightsAny tier of camp
02

ClassicThe route that works, done properly

The established circuits, built out of camps we return to. Shared game drives, scheduled light aircraft, and a plan that has been run enough times that we know where it strains. This is the tier most first safaris should be, and we will say so even when the alternative pays us more.

Shared vehicleScheduled flights7–10 nightsCamps and lodges
03

In the fieldWalking, fly-camping, further out

Mobile and walking safaris where the camp moves with you and the bathroom is a bucket. Physically real — we will ask about knees and heat before we quote. The reward is being the only vehicle, or no vehicle at all, for days.

Mobile campsWalking sectionsMin. age appliesSeasonal only
04

UltraExclusive use, private aircraft, no compromise

Whole camps taken out of inventory, private charter between them, and a team that travels with you. Used for families and groups who want the country without the other guests. At this level we are candid about what money cannot buy — weather, migration timing, and animals.

Exclusive usePrivate charterPrivate chefBespoke routing

Crux Safari™

The layer underneath all four
Intelligence layer

Crux Safari™

Routing, seasonality, guide matching and camp sequencing, run against the ledger rather than against a brochure.

It does not replace the person answering your email. It just means that person already knows the answer.

A safari falls apart in the joins — the transfer that does not connect, the camp booked in the wrong month, the guide who is excellent with cats and wasted on a birding trip.

Seasonality
Checks every camp against the month you are actually travelling, including the weeks either side of the obvious answer.
Routing
Sequences camps against real transfer times from the ledger and scheduled light-aircraft windows.
Guide matching
Pairs traveller interest with named guides we have been in a vehicle with.
Contrast
Builds deliberate change into the route — dry country then water, high ground then delta — so the days do not blur.
Honesty check
Flags where a request cannot be delivered as asked, before a quote goes out rather than after.
