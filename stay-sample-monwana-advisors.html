Why it is in the ledger

From experience

Thornybush is leopard country, and Monwana is the quiet end of it. It is small enough that the guide learns your name on the first drive, and good enough that you stop checking your phone by the second.

The lodge sits inside the Thornybush Nature Reserve, open to the Greater Kruger. Drives are private to the reserve, so sightings are not shared with a queue of vehicles. Afternoons are for the infinity pool and the waterhole; evenings for the firepit, the wine cellar and supper on the deck.

Monwana works with Thornybush on conservation and community work, and the Young Rangers programme gives children their own reason to be up at dawn.

Questions, answered plainly

Before you enquire
Is Monwana a good lodge for a first safari in South Africa?
Yes. Monwana sits inside the Thornybush Nature Reserve in the Greater Kruger, so you get dependable Big Five sightings on private game drives without self-driving or park crowds, plus a small lodge with private guides. It is a calm first safari.
Is Monwana good for couples and honeymooners?
Very. Suites open onto private decks over the bush or the waterhole, and days end with bush dining under the stars, wine tastings and stargazing. The house filmed Wild Together here, one couple's first safari.
Does Monwana work for families?
Yes. There are family suites, and children can join the Young Rangers programme at the waterhole. Tell us ages when you enquire and we will match the suite and the guide.
How do you get to Monwana?
Fly to Hoedspruit Eastgate Airport (HDS), then a 40-minute road transfer to the lodge. Transfers from Skukuza (SZK) or Kruger Mpumalanga International (MQP) are available on request.
When is the best time to visit Monwana?
May to September, the dry winter season, when wildlife gathers around water and Big Five viewing is strongest. October to April is green, with birds and young animals.
Has James Wild & Co stayed at Monwana?
Yes. James and Miriam stayed and vetted Monwana together, and filmed Wild Together there. Rates are confirmed for your dates at the time of enquiry.
