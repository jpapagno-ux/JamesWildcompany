The ship’s log

Day by day

Aliikai · Raja Ampat North

Sorong, outward
DayPlateAnchorageShip’s logNight
  1. 01SorongEmbarkation

    Met at the airport, twenty minutes to the harbour, lunch on deck as Aliikai clears Sorong. A check dive if the light allows; sundowners after.

    At anchor
  2. 02Kri PeninsulaSnorkel · Kayak

    Woken by birds in Mioskon Bay. Mangrove snorkelling, a kayak on mirrored water, the Kri reefs, and Mansuar village close by.

    At anchor
  3. 03The PassageBirding · Hike

    Up before dawn for the red bird-of-paradise with Arie, the cruise director. Then the mushroom islands of Kabui Bay and the tidal channel.

    At anchor
  4. 04Penemu IslandsLookout · Reef

    Limestone domes, a trail to the lookout everyone photographs, and reefs below with mantas and turtles.

    At anchor
  5. 05WayagClimb · Beach

    The view Raja Ampat is known for: a climb to the peak, then a deserted beach for the sunset.

    At anchor
  6. 06WofohPaddle · Coral wall

    Two islands joined by a shallow reef. Beachcombing, paddleboard and kayak, and a coral wall that drops into blue.

    At anchor
  7. 07ArborekMantas · Village

    Mantas feeding on the Dampier Strait current, then ashore to Arborek village.

    At anchor
  8. 08MansuarTurtles · Village

    Turtles and mantas in the morning, a village visit in the afternoon, sundowners as Aliikai sails for Batanta.

    At anchor
  9. 09Batanta & DayangTrek · Waterfalls

    A jungle trek to two waterfalls, an afternoon off Dayang Island, and the overnight run back to Sorong.

    Under way
  10. 10SorongDisembarkation

    A last breakfast on deck, then the crew sees you to the airport.

    Ashore
