A note before the curtain

The forward

Every travel company has a brochure. We have a ledger. One tells you where you could go. The other tells you where James has been.

What he slept on. Who picked up the phone. Whether he went back. Travelers stopped believing adjectives a while ago; they want proof, a host who answers, and enough nights to actually know a place.

That isn’t a trend we spotted. It’s how James has travelled since 1982. The rest of the industry is simply arriving — and they’re welcome. We kept the good table.

We won’t show you how the book works yet. Some things you earn by staying the third night.
