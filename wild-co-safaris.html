01Why These Platforms, Why Now

Safari routings have consolidated around the same operator decks.

Most itineraries advisors get back are assembled from a shared pool of camps and resold with different branding on top. The routing is templated even when the pitch isn't.

Wild & Co Safaris gives trade partners direct-relationship access to a smaller, firsthand-vetted set of camps and operators, with the reasoning behind each routing decision, not just a quote.

Entered into the Ledger because

Four tented suites on Osprey Lagoon, in the middle of two of the busiest elephant corridors in the Linyanti. It's the rare camp small enough to feel private while sitting on some of the best game viewing in northern Botswana, still under review until it's proven itself across more than one season.

Field Notes
  • Elephant herds cross the lagoon most mornings in the dry season
  • Take the barge for sunset, quieter than a drive
  • Shares Osprey Retreat spa and deli with sister camp DumaTau
  • Best window: June – October for elephant concentrations
Practical Notes
  • Access via light aircraft into the Linyanti Reserve
  • 4 tented suites only, books out fast in peak season
  • Each tent has a private plunge pool and deck
  • Rate basis: full board, activities included
If you want the bigger, more social energy of a larger camp, send them to sister camp DumaTau instead. Little DumaTau is quiet, small, and shares its facilities next door.
