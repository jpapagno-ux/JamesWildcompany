Why it is in the ledger

Recorded

Franschhoek is where Cape Town goes to eat. Leeu Estates is where it goes to stay when lunch turns into dinner and nobody wants to drive.

The estate sits on Dassenberg Road at the head of the Franschhoek Valley, a few minutes from the village. The Manor House and its terraces look across the vineyards to the mountains; the cottages give couples and families a door of their own. The Mullineux & Leeu wine studio, the Leeu Spa and the Everard Read gallery are on the grounds.

We picked it for the live-pricing sample because its rates sit on our booking engine, so the number you see is the number the engine would charge tonight.

Questions, answered plainly

Before you book
Are the prices on this page live?
Yes. When you change the dates, the page reads the booking engine for Leeu Estates and shows the Best Available Rate per room, per night, in US dollars, excluding taxes and fees. Results are cached for up to fifteen minutes.
Is the price shown the final price?
No. It is the nightly room rate before taxes and fees. The booking page shows the full total, and we confirm inclusions and policies with you before anything is charged.
How far is Leeu Estates from Cape Town?
About 70 km from Cape Town International Airport (CPT), roughly 60 to 75 minutes by car depending on traffic.
When is the best time to visit Franschhoek?
October to April: warm days, vineyards in leaf, harvest from late summer, and long lunches outdoors.
What is there to do at the estate?
Wine tastings at the Mullineux & Leeu wine studio, the Leeu Spa, the Everard Read gallery, the kitchen gardens, the Conservatory dining room and La Petite Colombe, and Franschhoek village a short drive away.
