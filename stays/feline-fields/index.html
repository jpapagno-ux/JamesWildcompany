Feline Fields

Two camps, one family, in northern Botswana: wet Delta at Khwai and dry Kalahari to the west, a 65-minute helicopter hop apart. James stayed at the Vintage Camp in 2026. The Lodge is its sister in the northern Kalahari, near the Namibian border. Both are a partner of the house.

Feline Fields Vintage Camp

I know a place · slept here, 2026
Where
Khwai, on the eastern edge of the Okavango Delta, Botswana
Access
Khwai Airstrip, about 20 minutes by road · from Maun 15 minutes by helicopter, 20 by light aircraft, or about 2½ hours by road
Tents
Eight bell tents with a king or twin bed and en-suite bathroom · two family tents, each with two bedrooms

Lounge, dining and wellness tents · bucket showers filled with warm water · a wooden hot tub · Victorian décor with Moroccan touches
Days
Morning and afternoon game drives · helicopter flights over the Delta · guided walks · mokoro on the channels · catch-and-release fishing · birding, with more than 450 species recorded · spa
Best for
Couples, families and private groups · birders and anglers

Mokoro levels hold steady through the year here, as they don't depend on the annual flood

?

Questions, answered plainly

Has James Wild stayed at Feline Fields?
Yes, at the Vintage Camp in Khwai, in 2026. The Lodge in the northern Kalahari is its sister camp and a partner of the house.
Where is Feline Fields?
In northern Botswana. The Vintage Camp is on the Khwai River at the eastern edge of the Okavango Delta; the Lodge is in the northern Kalahari, west of the Delta near the Namibian border.
How do you get there?
Fly into Maun. The Vintage Camp is 15 minutes by helicopter to Khwai Airstrip, then about 20 minutes by road; the Lodge is 50 minutes by helicopter from Maun. The two are 65 minutes apart by helicopter.
How do I book?
Write to james@jameswildco.com or call 561.479.6355 for a personal reply and dates priced for you.
