01Why These Platforms, Why Now

Advisors need a source they can stand behind.

Every OTA and AI itinerary tool now returns the same twelve properties, reworded. When a client asks "have you actually been here," too many advisors are quoting a listing back at them.

Wild Earth Stays gives trade partners a firsthand-only archive to recommend from: the dossier, the reasoning, and a name attached to every entry, so an advisor's recommendation carries the same weight as ours.

Forty-four years of firsthand travel, opened up to trade for the first time in 2026.

04Hand-Selected Partners

What it takes to enter the Ledger.

We work with a small number of properties, deliberately. Most properties considered for the Ledger don't make it in. Here's what we're actually looking for.

01
Character over category

A genuine point of view, not a checklist of amenities that reads the same as every other listing.

02
Firsthand only

We don't add anything we, or someone we trust completely, haven't experienced ourselves. No submissions, no press-trip fast-track.

03
Small and considered

Fewer than 100 entries total across the entire archive, added only when a place genuinely earns its place.

04
Direct relationships

We work straight with the property, no OTA-style booking layer sitting between us and where a client actually stays.

James Papagno has spent 44 years finding the places worth recommending, starting with a single-engine plane stuffed with surfboards for a one-week surf getaway to Eleuthera in 1982, and never really stopping since. He has personally stayed at the majority of properties recorded in the Ledger, returning to many of them repeatedly rather than checking them off once and moving on.

Wild Earth Stays is the result of that habit, formalized: a record built from firsthand time on the ground, not submissions, and not a site visit arranged around a press trip.
