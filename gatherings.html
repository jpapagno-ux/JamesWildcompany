What a resort residence is

The explanation

A family that travels together needs two things a hotel room cannot give it: enough space to stay under one roof, and enough help that nobody spends the week running the house.

A generational residence is built for three ages at once — grandparents, parents and children, with room for each to keep their own hours. A family residence is the same idea at a smaller scale: one household, one front door, nobody split across a corridor.

We hold them only at resorts. Not private villas, and not because villas are wrong — because a gathering works best when someone else is looking after the house. The resort brings the kitchens, the guides, the boats and the people who know the water. The residence keeps the family together.

02

How a gathering is built

Three parts
01 · The residence

One roof, every age.

Chosen for how it sleeps a family, not how it photographs. Bedrooms, shared rooms and outdoor space are recorded in the ledger before a house is offered.

02 · The resort

A team around you.

Dining, children’s days, wellness and housekeeping come from the resort, so the residence stays a home rather than a job.

03 · The gathering

The days, planned in.

Boats, offshore days, guided walks, a long table at the end of it — the experiences arranged around the family, with an advisor holding the plan.
