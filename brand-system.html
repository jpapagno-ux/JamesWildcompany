Entry No. 001The Brief

Why this exists.

Wild Earth Stays already has the hard part: decades of firsthand travel, real relationships with the camps and lodges it recommends, and a founder who has actually stayed in every property on the site. What the current brand doesn't do is show that. It reads like inventory: destinations, filters, an enquiry button.

The proposal is simple. Stop presenting places and start recording them. Give every property a dossier, not a listing: who visited, when, what they found, whether it earned its place. Let the site's own structure, the stamps, the entry numbers, the ship's-log formatting, do the work of proving this is personal, not templated.

Not corporate. Personal. Experience over inventory. Truthful, not flowery. Curated, not crowded.

Entry No. 004The Ledger Lanes

Four visual references, one system.

The wordmark, the document, the archive, the printed object: the same four lanes recur across every touchpoint so the brand reads as one continuous record, not a set of separate assets.

1 · The Wordmark

Authority through letterform

The name set alone, in Libre Caslon, with room to breathe. Authority through letterform and space. No icon required.

2 · The Document

Ruled and structured

Manifests, ship's logs, and ledger pages. Facts carry the story: Courier Prime, tables, and honest margins.

3 · The Archive

His negative, not stock

Photography from the field: grain over gloss, contact sheets and Polaroids over stock libraries.

4 · Print & Object

Uncoated. Stamped. Handled.

Luggage tags, wax seals, entry stamps: the physical objects the digital brand borrows its texture from.

Entry No. 005The Editorial System

Beyond stays, the same voice for every voyage.

Expeditions get the same treatment as camps: a ship's log instead of an itinerary, navigation notes instead of a packing list, sightings instead of stock inclusions.

Entry No. 006The Kill List

What we don't do.

Discipline is the brand. If a word or device shows up on every luxury travel site already, it doesn't belong on this one.

  • Drone shots
  • Couples in white linen at golden hour
  • Sage, terracotta, cream "eco-luxe" palettes
  • Compasses, antlers, mountain-line logos
  • Topographic contour patterns
  • Infinity pools
  • The word "wild" as an adjective
  • Unforgettable / magical / soul-stirring
  • Immersive / curated / iconic / hidden gem
  • Bucket list / once-in-a-lifetime
  • Breathtaking / stunning / spectacular
  • Anything we haven't experienced ourselves
