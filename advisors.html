Questions, answered plainly

Before you apply
Who is the Advisor Invitation for?
Independent travel advisors, agencies and consortia who want founder-vetted stays, safaris, voyages and family residences they can stand behind in front of a client.
What does an advisor receive?
Access to the ledger of recorded places with their vetting status, Field Guides for destinations, the Iggi and Safari CRUX guides, client Journey Folios, and the house webinar series, with a direct line to James.
How are commission and terms set?
Terms are shared on application and confirmed in an introductory call.
How do I apply?
Email james@jameswildco.com with your name, agency, host agency or consortium, IATA or CLIA number and the regions you sell. James replies personally.
