Log entry 00 · Before we begin

On 28 February 2020, James set sail for the Antarctic Peninsula aboard HX’s MS Roald Amundsen. By March, the COVID-19 shutdown was closing ports around the world, and the ship was left waiting in Antarctic waters.

This is that voyage, told by James himself and illustrated with his own photographs from the trip.
