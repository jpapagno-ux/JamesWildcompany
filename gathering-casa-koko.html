Casa Koko, Punta Mita

One house, nine bedrooms, twenty people and a surf break out front. James stayed here in September 2025 and knows the host, which means there’s a name to call, not a switchboard. And there’s one thing nearby he doesn’t put in brochures.

Why it is in the ledger

The whole family, one roof

Most family trips fail at dinner: three tables, two restaurants, one grandmother who wanted to stay in. Casa Koko solves it by being one house with one long table and a chef who already knows who doesn’t eat fish.

Nine en-suite bedrooms for up to twenty, on the oceanfront inside Punta Mita at the top of Banderas Bay. There’s a heated saltwater pool, a gym and spa, golf carts and bicycles for the children to commandeer, and a surf break directly in front for the ones who’d rather not be found.

A staff of twenty runs it, including a chef. The house lists babysitting on request, surf lessons, a family portrait session, and access to Punta Mita’s beach clubs and golf. Humpbacks come into the bay from mid-December to the end of March.

02 · James’s secret

Swimming with whale sharks. Where, he won’t say.

The largest fish in the sea, the gentlest thing in it, and a place James has swum himself and keeps off the internet on purpose. Arranged privately for guests of the house: a small boat, a guide in the water, and nobody else’s snorkel in your photograph.

  • Never touched, never chased. You swim beside them, not after them.
  • Small group, your group. The family, the guide and the shark. That’s the list.
  • Season decides. It runs when the sharks do. Ask with your dates and James will tell you straight.

Questions, answered plainly

Before you enquire
Is Casa Koko good for a family or multi-generational trip?
Yes. It is one house with nine en-suite bedrooms for up to twenty guests and a staff of twenty, including a chef. The house lists babysitting on request, surf lessons, golf carts and bicycles, and a family portrait session.
Where is Casa Koko?
On the oceanfront inside Punta Mita, on the Riviera Nayarit at the northern tip of Banderas Bay on Mexico’s Pacific coast, directly in front of one of Punta Mita’s surf breaks.
When can you see humpback whales from Casa Koko?
The house gives the humpback season as mid-December to the end of March, when whales come into Banderas Bay. Whale watching and private sailing are among the adventures the house arranges.
What is the whale shark swim, and where is it?
A private, small-group swim with whale sharks that James arranges for guests of the house. The location is not published. It depends on the season and your dates, so ask James and he will tell you whether they line up.
How long should we stay?
Three nights, at least. For a family of this size the first day is arrival, the second is settling in, and the third is the one they talk about. Ask about Another Day Together, the Gatherings Time Rewarded offer, where the house has one.
