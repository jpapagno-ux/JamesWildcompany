Straight answers

What people ask first

When is the best time for Raja Ampat?
Broadly October to April, when the seas are calmest and visibility is at its best. Komodo and Alor run the other way, April to November.
How do I get there?
Fly to Sorong for Raja Ampat or Labuan Bajo for Komodo, usually via Jakarta or Bali. The crew meets you at the airport.
Do I need to dive?
No. The snorkelling is world class, and non-divers kayak, paddleboard, hike to lookouts and visit villages. Divers get guided dives with Nitrox.
What does it cost?
From US$3,795 per person for five days in Komodo, US$4,830 for ten days in northern Raja Ampat, or the whole yacht at US$9,900 a night plus tax.
