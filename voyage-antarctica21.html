03

The vessel is part of the story.

Room to look outward.

05

Questions, answered plainly.

Before you enquire
Where does the Magellan Discoverer Inaugural Air-Cruise start and finish?

It starts and finishes in Punta Arenas, Chile. Guests sail south by ship through Southern Patagonia, Ushuaia and the Drake Passage, then fly back from King George Island to Punta Arenas on Day 9.

How long is the voyage?

Ten days and nine nights, following Antarctica21's published inaugural itinerary. Conditions determine the final programme.

What cabins does Magellan Discoverer have?

Five published categories, all with private balconies: Single Veranda Cabin (26 m²), Deluxe Veranda Cabin (26 m²), Grand Veranda Suite (38 m²), Family Veranda Suite (52 m²) and Triple Veranda Suite (52 m²).
