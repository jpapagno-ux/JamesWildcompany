Entry No. 0The Story So Far

Forty-four years, three platforms, one standard.

This didn't start as a company. It started as one person's notes on where to go back to, and it's stayed that way longer than it probably should have.

1982
Eleuthera

A one-week surf getaway to Eleuthera Island, Bahamas: a single-engine plane stuffed with surfboards, flown in from Miami. James never looked back. That first flight was the spark, and it led directly to founding Costa Rica's first surf travel company, still Central America's largest specialist operator in the category today.

1992
Adventure Expeditions

Expanded into owning and operating Adventure Expeditions, based in Boca Raton, Florida. It grew quickly into a wholesale travel company built around advisor-led journey design for eco and adventure travel.

2012
Africa

Expanded into Africa travel design. James has been designing safaris to Africa every year since.

2020
A Sale, Not an Exit

Sold Adventure Expeditions after nearly three decades, closing one chapter while the Africa and stays work kept growing.

2026
Two Platforms, One Founder

Wild Earth Stays and Wild & Co Safaris open as distinct platforms, still held to the same standard that started with a small plane full of surfboards in 1982.
