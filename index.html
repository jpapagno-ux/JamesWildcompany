The mission

Why this exists
For the trade

Travellers are not short of choice. They are short of certainty. Closing that gap, for the people who make the recommendation, is the entire reason this house exists.

44 years in the field Built for advisors

Advisors carry the risk of every recommendation they make. Most of what reaches them — brochures, decks, a rep's own sales trip — was never built to survive a client's actual arrival. We built the ledger, then the four portals on top of it, to give advisors something they can stand behind in front of a client.

Confidence and quality are what a traveller is actually buying, whether they say so or not. A photograph cannot supply either. A person who has been there, and a record that says so, can. Verify first, publish second, and never guess at either one — that is the model, and it has not changed in forty-four years.

James Wild Stays, Wild & Co Safaris, Wild & Co Voyages and Wild & Co Gatherings are four doors into the same archive — places to stay, guided African journeys, journeys by water and family residences at resorts — built for consortia, agencies and independent advisors who need to move fast without moving carelessly.

What the house is

The position
Summary

James Wild & Company is a travel house built on a single archive. Four consumer directions sit on top of it. The archive is the asset. The brands are how you reach it.

Field verified Est. 1982

We started in 1982 with a notebook, and the notebook never really stopped. Every place in the ledger has been visited by someone in this house — walked, slept in, eaten at, and written up the same evening while it was still accurate.

That sounds quaint until you look at what it produces. We hold 124 recorded properties across 19 countries, 456 individual room and suite records, and 3,045 rate observations verified in the last four days. We know which airstrip, how long the transfer really takes, which rooms are worth the upgrade and which ones back onto the generator.

What we do not have is an opinion on places we have not been. If you ask us about somewhere outside the ledger, we will tell you we have not been, and then we will go and find out. That is how entries get made.

What you will not find here

The house rules

Most of this industry sounds the same because it is all written by people who were never there. We keep a list of what we do not do, and we check our own work against it.

If you ever catch us using one of these, tell us. We will fix it. The list is not a joke — it is the reason our copy reads differently to everyone else's in the room.

  • Drone shots
  • Couples in white linen at golden hour
  • Sage, terracotta and cream eco-luxe palettes
  • Compasses, antlers and mountain-line logos
  • Topographic contour patterns
  • Infinity pools as a personality
  • The word wild used as an adjective
  • Unforgettable, magical, soul-stirring
  • Immersive, curated, iconic, hidden gem
  • Bucket list, once-in-a-lifetime
  • Breathtaking, stunning, spectacular
  • Anything we have not experienced ourselves
