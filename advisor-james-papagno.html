The field record

Dates, not adjectives
1982 · The house
Journey design begins. The seal still reads it.
1983 · Eleuthera
Friends ask to be taken surfing. Charlton meets the boat in North Eleuthera. Still his favourite island; still no queue.
1989 · Under sail
Starts sailing — surfing, diving and reading reefs along the way. Hasn’t stopped.
1996 · Tahiti
First of many seasons in the Society Islands. Taha’a becomes the one he names first.
2012 & 2018 · East & Southern Africa
Tswalu in the Kalahari; Singita Faru Faru, twice. The first entries marked I Keep Going Back.
2020 · Antarctica
Sails the Peninsula aboard HX’s MS Roald Amundsen as the world shuts down behind the ship. The Captain’s Log ›
2023 · Exumas & Moab
Reef work in the Exumas with the Reef Ball Foundation, staying at Kahari (now Baymahni); Ulum Moab with the family.
Nov 2024 · Raja Ampat
Ten days aboard Aliikai with his son. Still the richest reef he has dropped into.
2025 · Taha’a & Punta Mita
Vets Vahine Private Island; stays at Casa Koko, meets the host, swims with the whale sharks.
May 2026 · South Africa & Botswana
Monwana, Lion Sands Ivory, Kirkman’s Kamp, DumaTau, Mombo, Qorokwe — and every Leeu property walked on foot.
Next · Pencilled
Namibia’s Skeleton Coast and Kunene, Palawan by dive boat, and Vietnam’s Son Doong cave. The stamps change when he’s home.

04

What a Chief Expedition Officer does

Job description, unedited
  1. 01

    Goes first

    Nothing gets an oxblood stamp until he’s slept in it, walked it or swum it. Brochures don’t count. Neither do fam trips with a photographer.

  2. 02

    Keeps the book

    Every place gets a ledger ID, a mark and a date. When something changes, the old line is struck through in red, not deleted.

  3. 03

    Draws the route

    Contrast is the craft: Andes before the islands, the Winelands after the bush, a city night before the flight home.

  4. 04

    Says no

    To the wrong month, the wrong room and the wrong ship. Usually politely. Sometimes in the margin.

  5. 05

    Answers the phone

    A name, not a switchboard. If he hasn’t been, he’ll say so — and then he’ll go.

“I’m not sending you somewhere nobody’s been. There’s always somebody. I’m sending you where the right person has been — a year before the photographs.”

James Papagno · Chief Expedition Officer, ADV-2026-000001
